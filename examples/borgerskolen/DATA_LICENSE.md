licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence
(CC BY 4.0), <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>.

It combines:

- room temperatures and thermostat set-points measured in Borgerskolen,
  March 2021;
- outdoor temperature and global radiation from DMI station 06188
  (Sjaelsmark). Source: the Danish Meteorological Institute (DMI), open data,
  <https://opendatadocs.dmi.govcloud.dk/>, used under CC BY 4.0.
